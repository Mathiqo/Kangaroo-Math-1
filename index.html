2009 - Question 1

2 × 9 + 200 + 9 =

A) 292
B) 209
C) 290
D) 272
E) 227
Answer: 2009 - Question 1
Correct Answer: E) 227

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Calculate multiplication first: 2 × 9 = 18

2. Add 200: 18 + 200 = 218

3. Add 9: 218 + 9 = 227
