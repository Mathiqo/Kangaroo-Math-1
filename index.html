Question 1

2 × 9 + 200 + 9 =

A) 292
B) 209
C) 290
D) 272
E) 227
Answer to Question 1
Correct Answer: E) 227

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Calculate multiplication first: 2 × 9 = 18

2. Add 200: 18 + 200 = 218

3. Add 9: 218 + 9 = 227

Option E) 227

Question 4

In the picture you see the number 930. How many small squares must be changed so that the number becomes 806?

A) 5
B) 6
C) 7
D) 8
E) 9
Answer to Question 4
Correct Answer: C) 7

Step-by-Step Solution:

• Changing 9 to 8 requires turning 1 square on (bottom left): 1 change.

• Changing 3 to 0 requires turning 2 middle squares off and 2 side squares on: 4 changes.

• Changing 0 to 6 requires turning 1 top-right square off and 1 middle square on: 2 changes.

Total changes = 1 + 4 + 2 = 7 squares.

Question 5

Mother bought 16 mandarins. Karl ate half of them, Eva ate two, and Dana ate the rest. How many mandarins did Dana eat?

A) 4
B) 6
C) 8
D) 10
E) 12
Answer to Question 5
Correct Answer: B) 6

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Total mandarins = 16

2. Karl ate half = 16 ÷ 2 = 8 mandarins.

3. Eva ate = 2 mandarins.

4. Dana ate the rest = 16 - (8 + 2) = 16 - 10 = 6 mandarins.

Question 6

In his garden Tony made a pathway using 10 paving stones. Each paver was 4 dm wide and 6 dm long. He then drew a black line connecting the middle points of each paving stone. How long is the black line?

A) 24 dm
B) 40 dm
C) 46 dm
D) 50 dm
E) 56 dm
Answer to Question 6
Correct Answer: D) 50 dm

Step-by-Step Solution:

The line connects center points of 10 adjacent pavers laid lengthwise.

Total length = 10 × 6 dm = 60 dm. Subtracting half a paver length at both ends: 60 - 3 - 3 = 50 dm.

Question 7

Sophie rolled a die four times and scored a total of 23 points. How often did she roll a six?

A) 0
B) 1
C) 2
D) 3
E) 4
Answer to Question 7
Correct Answer: D) 3

Step-by-Step Solution:

• Maximum score in 4 rolls = 4 × 6 = 24.

• Sophie scored 23 (1 point short of maximum).

• Rolls must be 6 + 6 + 6 + 5 = 23.

She rolled a six 3 times.

Question 9

In a dance group there are 25 boys and 19 girls. Every week 2 more boys and 3 more girls join the group. After how many weeks will there be the same number of boys as girls in the dance group?

A) 6
B) 5
C) 4
D) 3
E) 2
Answer to Question 9
Correct Answer: A) 6

Step-by-Step Solution:

• Initial difference: 25 - 19 = 6 more boys.

• Girls gain 3 - 2 = 1 member per week on boys.

• Time needed = 6 ÷ 1 = 6 weeks.

Question 10

Peter shared a bar of chocolate. First he broke off a row with 5 pieces for his brother. Then he broke off a column with 7 pieces for his sister. How many pieces were there in the entire bar of chocolate?

A) 28
B) 32
C) 35
D) 40
E) 54
Answer to Question 10
Correct Answer: D) 40

Step-by-Step Solution:

• Row of 5 pieces means 5 columns.

• Remaining column of 7 pieces means original bar had 7 + 1 = 8 rows.

• Original pieces = 5 columns × 8 rows = 40 pieces.

Question 11

Question 13

B) 6 cm

Question 21

In a vase there is one red, one blue, one yellow and one white flower. Maja the bee visits each flower exactly once. She begins with the red flower and she never flies directly from the yellow to the white flower. In how many different ways can she visit each flower?

A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
E) 6
Answer to Question 21
Correct Answer: D) 4

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Total permutations starting with Red = 6.

2. Subtract 2 invalid paths (Yellow directly followed by White).

3. Valid paths = 6 - 2 = 4 ways.

Question 22

A) 17:00
B) 17:45
C) 18:30
D) 19:00
E) 19:15
Answer to Question 22
Correct Answer: A) 17:00

Step-by-Step Solution:

2. Backward counting clock: 18:15 - 1 hr 15 min = 17:00.
